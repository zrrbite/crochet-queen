# crochet-queen

Crochet patterns written out as text, plus the know-how they rely on.

Videos are great for seeing a stitch, but hard to follow mid-round. Each
recipe here is a video (or other source) turned into a written pattern you can
keep next to you while you work, with a link back to the original.

## Layout

```
knowledge/              how to crochet, independent of any one pattern
  reading-patterns.md   abbreviations, notation, US vs UK terms
  stitches/             how to make each stitch
  techniques/           methods: magic ring, working in the round, finishing
  materials/            yarn weights, hook sizes, fibres
recipes/                patterns, one file each, sorted by type
  TEMPLATE.md           copy this to start a new recipe
  amigurumi/            the cute ones: stuffed bears, frogs, fruit
  accessories/          things to wear or carry
  appliques/            small flat motifs to sew or stick on
  blankets/             blankets and the squares that make them
  clothing/             sweaters and cardigans
  home/                 things for the house
videos.md               every video used or considered, tagged for searching
```

Looking for the next project? [videos.md](videos.md) lists every video a
recipe came from and the other candidates found along the way, each tagged
with type, level, yarn and whether a free written pattern exists, so you can
search it for "no-sew" or "intermediate" or "fingering".

Recipe types. A folder is added when the first pattern of that type arrives:

| Folder         | For                                                       |
| -------------- | --------------------------------------------------------- |
| `amigurumi/`   | stuffed toys and little characters: bears, frogs, fruit   |
| `accessories/` | hats, bags, scrunchies, scarves, headbands                |
| `appliques/`   | hearts, flowers, stars: to sew on, or as charms and garlands |
| `blankets/`    | blankets, throws, and the squares that make them          |
| `home/`        | coasters, dishcloths, baskets, cushions, wall hangings    |
| `clothing/`    | sweaters, cardigans, tops                                 |

## Conventions

- **US terms** throughout (`sc`, `hdc`, `dc`). If a source uses UK terms,
  convert them; [reading-patterns.md](knowledge/reading-patterns.md) has the
  table. Say in the recipe which terms the source used.
- **Stitch count at the end of every round**, after a dash: `— 20 sts`.
- **Recipes link to knowledge, not the other way round.** A recipe says
  "make a magic ring" and links to how. A technique the recipe uses in an
  unusual way gets a note in the recipe itself.
- **Credit the source.** Every recipe names the designer and links the video
  or page it came from. The wording here is ours; the design is theirs.
- **Mark anything not taken from the source**, such as an estimated size, so
  it isn't mistaken for the designer's numbers.
- **Every recipe opens with a preview image.** It's the source video's own
  thumbnail, loaded from YouTube and linking to the video, so nothing of the
  designer's is copied into this repo. When one of ours gets made, a photo of
  it can take the thumbnail's place.

## Recipes

### Amigurumi

- [Waffle the bear](recipes/amigurumi/waffle-the-bear.md): a chubby
  one-piece bear with ears, arms and a muzzle. Easy, a good second
  amigurumi. From Little World of Whimsy.
- [Leggy frog](recipes/amigurumi/leggy-frog.md): squishy, long-legged, and
  no sewing. Beginner. From Anita Louise Crochet.
- [Strawberry](recipes/amigurumi/strawberry.md): a small stuffed berry with
  leaves and seeds; makes a keychain. Beginner. From Crochet with Mai.
- [Axolotl](recipes/amigurumi/axolotl.md): one piece from nose to tail, with
  chain gills, bobble legs and a surface-crochet fin; nothing sewn.
  Intermediate. From CrochetByGenna.

### Accessories

- [Bucket hat](recipes/accessories/bucket-hat.md): double crochet, sized by
  measuring as you go. Beginner. From VivCrochets.
- [Scrunchie](recipes/accessories/scrunchie.md): ruffles crocheted onto a
  hair tie. Beginner. From ElyseCrochets.
- [Winter Ice shawl](recipes/accessories/winter-ice-shawl.md): a one-skein
  triangular lace shawl in fingering yarn, where blocking is half the work.
  Experienced. From Carmen of Annie Design Crochet.

### Appliques

- [Mini heart](recipes/appliques/mini-heart.md): one round into a magic
  ring. Beginner. From ElyseCrochets.
- [Simple flower](recipes/appliques/simple-flower.md): five petals of treble
  crochet. Beginner. From Pora Pora Crochet.

### Blankets

- [Granny square](recipes/blankets/granny-square.md): the classic square.
  Beginner. From Maddy Marie.

### Clothing

- [Two-tone raglan sweater](recipes/clothing/raglan-sweater.md): a top-down
  raglan in plain dc, sized by trying it on, with post-stitch ribbing at the
  collar, cuffs and hem. Intermediate. From VivCrochets.

### Home

- [Flat circle coaster](recipes/home/flat-circle-coaster.md): five rounds
  of double crochet, beginner. From Crochet and Tea.
