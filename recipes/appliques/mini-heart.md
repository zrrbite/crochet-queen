# Mini heart

A small flat heart worked entirely into one magic ring. Tall stitches on the
two sides make the top bumps, one treble makes the point, and pulling the
ring shut draws it all into a heart. One round, a few minutes. Use it as a
charm, sew it onto a bag or hat, or string several into a garland.

## Materials

- A little worsted-weight yarn (any yarn works, with a hook to match)
- 4 mm hook
- Scissors, tapestry needle (optional)

## Gauge

Not needed.

## Stitches and techniques

- [Magic ring](../../knowledge/techniques/magic-ring.md): essential here,
  since closing it is what shapes the heart
- [Chain, slip stitch, double crochet, treble crochet](../../knowledge/stitches/basic-stitches.md)
- [Weaving in ends](../../knowledge/techniques/finishing.md#weaving-in-ends)

## Before you start

Every stitch goes **into the magic ring**, not into other stitches. Keep the
ring loose enough to fit them all, and don't pull it shut until the end.

## Pattern

**Start:** make a magic ring. Ch 4. This chain doesn't count as a stitch; it
makes the curve at the top of the first bump.

**Into the ring, in order:**

1. 3 tr: first bump
2. 3 dc: side, sloping down
3. ch 1
4. 1 tr: the bottom point
5. ch 1
6. 3 dc: other side, sloping up
7. 3 tr: second bump

**Close:** ch 4, then sl st into the ring. Pull the starting tail firmly: the
ring closes, the two ch-4s meet in a dip at the top, and the heart forms.

**Fasten off:** cut the yarn and pull the tail through the loop. Tie the two
tails together on the back, weave them in, and trim.

## Variations

- *(Ours)* **Bigger heart:** use thicker yarn and a bigger hook; the stitch
  sequence doesn't change.
- *(Ours)* **Garland:** chain a long string, then join hearts along it with a
  sl st through the dip of each.
- *(Ours)* **A heart for the bear:** make one in the same sport-weight yarn
  as [Waffle the bear](../amigurumi/waffle-the-bear.md), on a smaller hook, so
  it's in scale. Sew it between the arms.
