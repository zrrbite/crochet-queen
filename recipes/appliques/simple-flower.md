# Simple flower

A flat five-petal flower worked in one round into a chain ring, each petal
a little fan of treble crochets. It leaves an open centre you can fill with a
button, a bead or a French knot. Sew it onto a hat, a bag, a granny square
or a hair clip.

## Materials

- A little worsted-weight yarn
- 4.25 mm hook (4 mm or 4.5 mm is fine)
- Tapestry needle, scissors

## Gauge

Not needed.

## Stitches and techniques

- [Slip knot, chain, slip stitch, treble crochet](../../knowledge/stitches/basic-stitches.md)
- [Magic ring: the chain-ring alternative](../../knowledge/techniques/magic-ring.md#alternative-chain-ring)
- [Weaving in ends](../../knowledge/techniques/finishing.md#weaving-in-ends)

## Pattern

**Start:** ch 6, sl st into the first ch to make a ring.

**Petals:** \*ch 4, 3 tr into the ring, ch 4, sl st into the ring\*; rep from
\* 5 times in all. — 5 petals

Each petal rises on the first ch 4, fans out with the 3 tr, and comes back
down on the second ch 4 to the sl st. Space the petals evenly round the ring
as you go, sliding them along if they bunch up.

**Fasten off:** cut the yarn and pull it through. Weave both ends in on the
back, through the base of the petals.

## Notes

- **Five or six petals?** The video's caption says to repeat the petal
  6 times, but the flower made in the video has 5 petals, and so does the
  video's description. Six repeats also works and makes a fuller flower with
  petals that overlap a little.
- *(Ours, untested)* **Two colours:** in a centre colour, work 10 sc into the
  ring and join with a sl st. Fasten off, join the petal colour with a sl st
  in any sc, then [ch 4, 3 tr in the next st, ch 4, sl st in the next st]
  5 times.
