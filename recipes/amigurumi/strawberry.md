# Strawberry

A small stuffed strawberry: a red berry worked from the tip up in eleven
rounds, a green star of leaves sewn on top, and white seeds stitched on.
About ten minutes of crochet once you know the stitches. Makes a nice
keychain or bag charm.

## Materials

- Milk cotton (or any cotton or acrylic DK) in red, green and white: scraps
  are enough
- 3 mm hook
- A little fibre fill
- Tapestry needle, scissors, 1 stitch marker

Pink or pale red make lovely strawberries too.

## Gauge

Not critical. Work tightly so the stuffing doesn't show.

## Stitches and techniques

- [Single crochet, chain, slip stitch](../../knowledge/stitches/basic-stitches.md)

## Before you start

- The berry is worked in **continuous rounds** (a spiral), starting at the
  pointy tip. Mark the last stitch of each round.
- Early rounds increase in **3 places**, which grows a cone rather than a flat
  circle. That's what gives the strawberry its point.

## Berry (red)

**R1:** 6 sc in a magic ring. — 6 sts

**R2:** [1 sc, inc] 3 times. — 9 sts

**R3:** [2 sc, inc] 3 times. — 12 sts

**R4:** [3 sc, inc] 3 times. — 15 sts

**R5:** [4 sc, inc] 3 times. — 18 sts

**R6:** [2 sc, inc] 6 times. — 24 sts

**R7–8:** sc in each st around (2 rounds). — 24 sts

**R9:** [2 sc, dec] 6 times. — 18 sts

**R10:** [1 sc, dec] 6 times. — 12 sts

**Stuff** the berry firmly.

**R11:** dec 6 times. — 6 sts

**Close:** cut a long tail. Thread it on the needle and pass it through the
**front loops only** of the last 6 stitches, then pull to close the hole.
Weave in the end.

## Leaves (green)

**R1:** 6 sc in a magic ring, pull closed. Sl st into the first sc.

**Leaves:** [ch 4. Starting in the 2nd ch from the hook, 1 sc in each of the
3 ch back down the chain. Sl st into the next sc of the ring] 6 times, once
for each stitch of the ring.

Ch 1 and fasten off, leaving a tail to sew with.

## Finishing

1. **Leaves:** centre the leaves over the closed top of the berry and sew them
   on with the green tail, stitching around the middle so the leaf tips stay
   free.
2. **Seeds:** with white yarn on the needle, make short single stitches, each
   about one stitch long, scattered all over the berry. Bring the needle out
   where the next seed goes, so the strand travels inside the berry.
3. Bury all the ends inside and trim.

## Tips

- *(Ours, not the source's)* To make a keychain, sew a jump ring or a loop of
  yarn into the centre of the leaves before you sew them down.
- *(Ours)* Seeds look most natural spaced evenly but not in lines; slant them
  all the same way.
