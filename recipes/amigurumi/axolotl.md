# Axolotl (no sew)

<a href="https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=oGlR-bIaxbY"><img src="https://img.youtube.com/vi/oGlR-bIaxbY/maxresdefault.jpg" alt="Two crocheted axolotls, pink and mint green, with frilly gills, held in a hand over a lawn; on the right the same two from the side, showing the fin along the tail" width="480"></a>

*The video's thumbnail; click to watch.*

A small axolotl in chenille yarn, worked in one piece from the nose to the
tip of the tail. The six gills are crocheted straight off the head in a
contrast colour, the four legs are bobble stitches on the belly, the tail is
cinched shut, and the fin along the tail is crocheted onto the surface at the
end. Nothing is sewn. It's a step up from the bear and the frog because of
the gill round: you change colour one stitch ahead every few stitches, work
little chains out and back, and then have to crochet past the gills without
catching them. The bobbles and the surface-crochet fin are new too, but
easy.

## Materials

- Super bulky chenille yarn: a main colour for the body and a contrast colour
  for the gills and tail fin. The designer lists one skein of each; the
  contrast colour only does the gills and the fin, so a leftover is enough
  for it (Ours).
- 5 mm hook
- 2 × 12 mm safety eyes
- Polyester fibre fill
- 1 or 2 stitch markers
- Tapestry needle (the designer also uses a thicker one to poke yarn ends
  inside), scissors

**Safety eyes are not safe for small children**: they can come out and be
swallowed. For an axolotl going to a baby or toddler, embroider the eyes
with black yarn or use felt.

## Gauge

Not critical. The size above is for chenille on a 5 mm hook; the fabric must
be tight enough that no stuffing shows between stitches.

## Before you start

- **Continuous rounds** (a spiral). Put the marker in the last stitch of
  each round and move it up.
- One piece, nose to tail: the magic ring is the tip of the nose. Head with
  gills (R1–9), neck, body with legs (R10–16), tail (R17–22), cinch shut.
  Eyes and head stuffing go in after R11, while you can still reach in. The
  tail fin is added last.
- **How the no-sew works here.** Each gill is a short chain worked out from
  the head and crocheted straight back down, so it's anchored where it
  starts. Each leg is a bobble: four dc into one stitch, gathered at the top
  into a bump. The fin is a line of hdc worked through the fabric of the
  finished tail.
- `inc` = 2 sc in the same stitch. `dec` = decrease over the next 2 stitches;
  the designer just says "decrease", so sc2tog or the invisible decrease,
  as you prefer.
- **Bobble (BO):** [yo, insert the hook in the stitch, yo, pull up a loop,
  yo, pull through 2 loops] 4 times into the same stitch, so there are 5
  loops on the hook. Yo, pull through all 5. One bobble counts as 1 stitch.
  A bobble pops out towards the back of the work, which here is the
  inside; push it through to the outside with a finger.
- **Changing colour one stitch ahead.** Everywhere the pattern says "switch
  to X as you finish this stitch", work the stitch until two loops are left
  on the hook, then yo with X and pull it through. The next stitch starts
  cleanly in X. Carry the colour you aren't using loosely on the inside; in
  R6 the gaps are only one stitch wide.
- **Gill (contrast colour), used six times in R6:**

  > Ch 5. Sc in the 2nd ch from the hook and in each of the next 3 ch
  > (4 sc, working back down the chain to the head), switching to the main
  > colour as you finish the 4th sc. Then 1 sc into the next stitch of the
  > head in the main colour.

  The skipped chain is the gill's tip. Every gill uses up one head stitch
  (the sc after it), and the stitch between two gills is in the main colour,
  so only the gills themselves show the contrast.

## Head (main colour)

**R1:** 6 sc in a magic ring, pull closed. — 6 sts

**R2:** inc in each st around. — 12 sts

**R3:** [1 sc, inc] 6 times. — 18 sts

**R4:** [2 sc, inc] 6 times. — 24 sts

**R5:** sc in each st around. — 24 sts

**R6 (gills):** 5 sc, switching to the contrast colour as you finish the 5th.
Gill, switching to the contrast colour as you finish its last sc (the one in
the head). Gill, switching again. Gill, this time finishing its last sc in
the main colour. 9 sc, switching to the contrast colour as you finish the
9th. Gill, switch. Gill, switch. Gill, finishing in the main colour. 4 sc.
— 24 sts (head stitches; the gills aren't counted)

Count: 5 + 3 + 9 + 3 + 4 = 24. The two sets of three gills sit on opposite
sides of the head. Cut the contrast yarn, leaving a tail; it isn't needed
again until the fin. Tie that tail to the magic ring's tail on the inside and
trim both short.

**R7:** sc in each st around, into the head stitches only. Keep the gills to
the outside as you pass them, so none gets crocheted into the body.
— 24 sts

**R8:** [2 sc, dec] 6 times. — 18 sts

**R9:** [1 sc, dec] 6 times. — 12 sts

That's the neck.

## Body (main colour)

**R10:** [1 sc, inc] 6 times. — 18 sts

**R11 (front legs):** 7 sc, bobble, 4 sc, bobble, 5 sc. — 18 sts

The stitch right after a bobble hides behind it; find it before you work it.
Push both bobbles out to the outside. From below you now have two front feet.

**Eyes and head stuffing, now.** Put the 12 mm eyes **between R4 and R5**,
counting rounds out from the magic ring, **about 8 stitches apart**: one on
each side of the face, roughly in line with the gap between the top gill and
the middle one on that side. Look at it from the front and check they're
level before pushing the washers on from the inside. Then stuff the head
firmly through the opening; it gets hard to reach later. The gills tend to
curl: press their tips flat.

**R12–14:** sc in each st around (3 rounds). — 18 sts

Don't double-count when you go over a bobble; a bobble is one stitch.

**R15 (back legs):** 8 sc, bobble, 4 sc, bobble, 4 sc. — 18 sts

One stitch further round than R11, which keeps the back legs lined up with
the front ones as the spiral drifts (Ours).

**R16:** sc in each st around. — 18 sts

**Stuff the body** now, firmly. The next round starts the tail and the hole
only gets smaller.

## Tail (main colour)

**R17:** [1 sc, dec] 6 times. — 12 sts

**R18:** sc in each st around. — 12 sts

**R19:** [1 sc, dec] 4 times. — 8 sts

Top up the stuffing; from here on stuff as you go.

**R20:** sc in each st around. — 8 sts

**R21:** [1 sc, dec] 2 times, 2 sc. — 6 sts

Last chance to add stuffing.

**R22:** sc in each st around. — 6 sts

**Close:** take the marker out, cut the yarn with a long tail and thread it
on the needle. Pass it through the front loops of the 6 stitches of R22 and
pull tight to cinch the tip shut. Weave the rest of the tail into the body,
trim it, and poke what's left inside with a thick needle.

## Face

Only the safety eyes, placed after R11 (above). The designer adds no mouth or
cheeks.

## Tail fin (contrast colour, optional)

The fin is surface crochet: hdc worked through the fabric of the stuffed
tail, not into stitches. Each hdc goes:

> Yo, push the hook through the tail a stitch further along, yo and pull a
> loop back up through the fabric (3 loops on the hook), yo and pull through
> all 3.

1. Start on top of the tail **between R19 and R20**, where the tail begins.
   Push the hook through, pull up a loop of the contrast colour, and make a
   sl st to secure it.
2. Hdc along the ridge of the tail towards the tip, one hdc per stitch.
   Chenille is dense, so wiggle the hook to get it through.
3. Keep going round the tip and back along the underside, and stop when
   you're level with where you started.
4. Cut the yarn, pull the end through, and weave it into the body, coming
   out somewhere else. Weave the starting tail in to the same spot, knot the
   two together, trim, and poke the knot inside with a thick needle.

## Finishing

Nothing to sew. Check every yarn end is inside and press the gills flat
again if they've curled.

## Tips from the source

- **R6 is the hard round.** Change colour as you finish the stitch *before*
  the new colour, every time, and the single stitch between two gills comes
  out in the body colour.
- **R7: crochet past the gills, not into them.** Fold each gill to the
  outside and work only into the head stitches behind it.
- **The stitch after a bobble is hidden.** Find it before you work it, and
  don't count a bobble twice on the rounds that follow.
- **Stuff the head at R11 and the body at R16**, before the decreases. After
  that, stuff as you go; the tail opening gets small fast.
- **Gills curl on their own.** Press the tips flat whenever you notice.
- Two contrasting colours look best: one for the body, one for the gills and
  fin.

## Variations

- Any two colours. The thumbnail shows pink with darker pink gills, and mint
  with green.
- Skip the fin; the designer calls it optional.
- A different yarn and hook changes the size; the shape is the same (Ours,
  untested).
- A short stitch of black yarn under the eyes for a smile, done while the
  body is still open so the ends tie off inside (Ours, untested).
