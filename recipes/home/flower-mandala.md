# Flower mandala

<a href="https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=CeDCbr0DkR4"><img src="https://img.youtube.com/vi/CeDCbr0DkR4/maxresdefault.jpg" alt="A lacy ten-petalled mandala in yellow, green, blue, red and peach, stretched on a yarn-wrapped metal hoop" width="480"></a>

*The video's thumbnail; click to watch.*

A layered, lacy mandala with ten petals, built up in 19 joined rounds, each
in a new colour. Flowers of popcorns, scallops and clusters sit on top of one
another, and the outer rounds make pointed petals with chain loops between
them. Designed by Sanita Brensone; this write-up follows Luna Crochet Designs'
tutorial of her pattern. It's intermediate because it uses most of the stitch
family and then some: popcorns, three sizes of cluster, front- and back-post
stitches at three heights, back loops, the third loop, and a long stitch that
reaches down four rounds. Most rounds work into spaces or into stitches a
round or more below rather than into the stitch in front of you, and every
round starts a fresh colour with a standing stitch. Nothing in it is hard by
itself; there are just 19 different repeats to keep straight. Finished, it's
stretched onto a metal hoop and hung on a wall, or used flat as a table mat.

## Materials

- DK yarn, about 80 g in total. The tutorial uses odd balls from the stash,
  a new colour every round, and recommends YarnArt Jeans if you're buying.
  Any number of colours works; see variations for one-ball versions.
- 4 mm hook
- A metal hoop about 10 cm wider than the finished mandala (50 cm for the DK
  version), if it's going on a wall
- Tapestry needle, scissors, a few stitch markers

The tutorial doesn't name its colours. Read from the video, it rotates six
shades: aqua, light grey, royal blue, teal, lime green and sky blue. Per round

## Before you start

- **Ten petals.** Every round is the same thing repeated 10 times. "A petal"
  below means one repeat. Count repeats, not stitches. A marker at the tip of
  one petal from R10 on tells you where you are for the rest *(ours)*.
- **Joined rounds, right side facing, never turn.** Every round ends with a
  sl st into its first stitch; then fasten off and cut the yarn. The next
  colour starts fresh wherever that round's line says, with a standing
  stitch.
- **Standing stitch:** put a slip knot on the hook, then work the stitch as
  normal (for a dc, yarn over first, then insert). It replaces the starting
  chain, so there's nothing to count as a stitch and no ch-3 to join into. A
  standing popcorn is a popcorn whose first dc is standing; a standing
  cluster has a standing first leg. If you're working in one colour and would
  rather not cut, replace the standing stitch with a chain of the right
  height and carry on *(ours)*.
- **Spaces vs stitches.** "In the ch-2 sp" means the hook goes under the
  chain, into the gap. "In the next st" means into the top of a stitch.
- **Reaching down.** Several rounds (3, 5, 8, 14, 19) work into or around
  stitches one to four rounds below, in front of or behind the rounds since.
  Each line says which stitch and where it is.
- **Post stitches:** FPdc and BPdc are in the knowledge page; FPsc, BPsc and
  FPtr are the same hook path at sc and tr height. "Around a cluster" means
  the cluster's body is the post: hook in beside it from the front, out the
  other side.
- **Clusters:** `dc3tog` is three dc each stopped one step early (two loops
  left of each), then one yarn over through all four loops on the hook.
  `tr3tog` is the same with trebles; `dc5tog` with five dc (six loops). When a
  cluster goes "in a space", all its legs go into that space. One leg can be a
  post stitch, which R9 uses.
- **BLO** means the back loop of the stitch top only. **Third loop:** on the
  back of a dc or sc, just below the two top loops, there's a third horizontal
  strand. Tip the work towards you to see it. Working into it only leaves the
  two top loops lying across the front as a neat ridge. R17 does this.
- **Long sc:** a sc whose insertion is far below the working round. Pull the
  first loop up to the height of the round you're on before finishing it, or
  the fabric puckers.
- **Counts.** Clusters and popcorns count as one stitch each. Spaces are
  listed separately where the next round works into them.

## Pattern

**R1** (aqua): make a magic ring. Ch 5 (counts as tr + ch 1). \*Tr into the
ring, ch 1\*; rep from \* 9 times in all. Pull the ring closed. Sl st into
the 4th ch of the ch 5 to join. Fasten off. — 10 tr, 10 ch-1 sp

**R2** (light grey): standing sc in any ch-1 sp, ch 2, 3 FPdc around the next
tr. \*Sc in next ch-1 sp, ch 2, 3 FPdc around next tr\*; rep from \* around.
Sl st to the first sc. Fasten off. — 40 sts (10 sc, 30 FPdc), 10 ch-2 sp.
The FPdc groups stand up as ten small petals.

**R3** (royal blue): worked behind R2, into the tops of the R1 trebles. Tug
each tr a little to find its top, and don't go into the ch-1 beside it.
Standing sc in the top of any tr, ch 3, \*sc in top of next tr, ch 3\*; rep
from \* around. Sl st to the first sc. Fasten off. — 10 sc, 10 ch-3 sp

**R4** (teal): standing dc in any ch-3 sp, dc, ch 1, 2 dc in the same sp.
\*2 dc, ch 1, 2 dc in next ch-3 sp\*; rep from \* around. Sl st to the first
dc. Fasten off. — 40 dc, 10 ch-1 sp

**R5** (lime green): standing popcorn (5 dc) in any ch-1 sp. \*Ch 3, FPtr
around the next R3 sc (it's two rounds down, between this dc group and the
next), ch 3, popcorn in next ch-1 sp\*; rep from \* until you're back at the
first popcorn; the last repeat stops after the ch 3 that follows its FPtr.
Sl st to the top of the first popcorn. Fasten off.
— 20 sts (10 popcorns, 10 FPtr), 20 ch-3 sp

**R6** (royal blue): each ch-3 sp gets four stitches, rising towards the
popcorn and falling away from it, so each popcorn ends up on the crest of a
scallop. Start in a ch-3 sp that comes *after* a popcorn (popcorn, space,
FPtr): standing dc, dc, hdc, sc in that sp. \*Sc, hdc, 2 dc in the next sp
(the one before a popcorn); skip the popcorn; 2 dc, hdc, sc in the next sp\*;
rep from \* until one space is left, the one before the first popcorn; sc,
hdc, 2 dc in it. Sl st to the first dc. Fasten off. — 80 sts

**R7** (teal), BLO throughout: start in any hdc whose next stitch is a dc
(the hdc on the near side of a 2-dc pair). Standing hdc BLO there. \*2 dc BLO
in each of the next 4 dc (passing the popcorn between the pairs), hdc BLO in
the next hdc, skip 2 sc, hdc BLO in the next hdc\*; rep from \* around,
leaving out the last hdc on the last repeat. Sl st to the first hdc. Fasten
off. — 100 sts (10 petals of hdc, 8 dc, hdc). Skipping the sc pairs draws the
scallops together; the lime FPtr now sits under each gap.

**R8** (sky blue): standing FPdc around any R5 FPtr. It's three rounds down,
under the gap between two petals; the stitch is tall. \*BPsc around each of
the next 10 sts (hdc, 8 dc, hdc), FPdc around the next R5 FPtr\*; rep from \*
around, leaving out the last FPdc. Sl st to the first FPdc. Fasten off.
— 110 sts. Back post sc are fiddlier than front post; take your time.

**R9** (light grey): count the 10 BPsc of a petal 1 to 10, from the FPdc.
Standing dc3tog over BPsc 3, 4 and 5. \*Ch 5, dc3tog over BPsc 6, 7 and 8,
ch 4, corner cluster: dc3tog with the 1st leg in BPsc 9, the 2nd leg worked as
a front post leg around the R8 FPdc, and the 3rd leg in BPsc 2 of the next
petal; ch 4, dc3tog over BPsc 3, 4 and 5 of that petal\*; rep from \* around,
leaving out the last cluster. Sl st to the top of the first cluster. Fasten
off. — 30 clusters, 10 ch-5 sp (petal tips), 20 ch-4 sp.
*On the corner cluster the tutorial says "next stitch", then "skip one" only
on the far side of the FPdc. By its own counts BPsc 10 is passed over too, so
we read the corner as centred on the FPdc with one BPsc unworked on each side
(ours).*

**R10** (sky blue): standing FPdc around the first cluster of any petal (the
one worked over BPsc 3–5). \*5 dc, ch 3, 5 dc in the ch-5 sp; FPdc around the
next cluster; 2 sc in the next ch-4 sp, 2 sc in the next ch-4 sp (the corner
cluster between them is passed); FPdc around the next cluster\*; rep from \*
around, leaving out the last FPdc. Sl st to the first FPdc. Fasten off.
— 160 sts, 10 ch-3 sp. Ten pointed petals with a chain tip.

**R11** (lime green): standing BPsc around the FPdc before any petal (the
one followed by 5 dc). \*BPsc around each of the next 5 dc, ch 2, BPsc around
each of the next 6 sts (5 dc, FPdc), ch 6, skip the 4 sc, BPsc around the next
FPdc\*; rep from \* around, leaving out the last BPsc. Sl st to the first
BPsc. Fasten off. — 120 sts, 10 ch-2 sp, 10 ch-6 sp. The back post stitches
push the R10 petals forward; their ch-3 tips stay loose in front for now, and
get caught in R14.

**R12** (royal blue): count the 6 BPsc on the first side of a petal 1 to 6,
from the FPdc. Standing sc in BPsc 4, sc in BPsc 5 and 6. \*3 sc in the ch-2
sp, sc in each of the next 3 BPsc, skip 3 BPsc, 9 dc in the ch-6 sp, skip 3
BPsc, sc in each of the next 3 BPsc\*; rep from \* around, leaving out the
last 3 sc. Sl st to the first sc. Fasten off. — 180 sts (10 × 9 sc, 9 dc).
The dc fans sit between petals; the sc run over each petal tip.

**R13** (sky blue), sc in BLO: count the 9 sc of a petal 1 to 9. Standing sc
BLO in sc 4, 2 sc BLO in sc 5, sc BLO in sc 6. \*Skip 3 sc, dc in the first
dc, [ch 1, dc in next dc] 8 times, skip 3 sc, sc BLO in sc 4, 2 sc BLO in
sc 5, sc BLO in sc 6\*; rep from \* around, leaving out the last 4 sc. Sl st
to the first sc. Fasten off. — 130 sts (40 sc, 90 dc), 80 ch-1 sp

**R14** (teal): count the 4 sc of a petal 1 to 4; the R10 petal tip is
sticking up in front, between sc 2 and 3. Standing sc BLO in sc 2. Long sc:
push the hook down through the ch-3 tip of that R10 petal, four rounds below
and in front of everything since, pull the loop up to this round's height,
finish the sc. Sc BLO in sc 3; don't skip anything between the long sc and
this one. \*Ch 1, [BPdc around the next dc, ch 1] 9 times, sc BLO in sc 2 of
the next petal, long sc through its R10 petal tip, sc BLO in sc 3\*; rep from
\* around, leaving out the last 3 sc. Sl st to the first sc. Fasten off.
— 120 sts (10 × sc, long sc, sc, 9 BPdc), 100 ch-1 sp

**R15** (lime green): count the 10 ch-1 sp of a petal 1 to 10, from the sc
group. The hardest round to remember; keep this line beside you. Standing sc
in the top of any long sc. \*Ch 3, skip sp 1, dc3tog in sp 2, ch 2, dc3tog in
sp 3, ch 2, dc3tog in sp 4, ch 3, tr3tog in sp 5, ch 4, tr3tog in sp 6, ch 3,
dc3tog in sp 7, ch 2, dc3tog in sp 8, ch 2, dc3tog in sp 9, ch 3, skip sp 10,
sc in the next long sc\*; rep from \* around, leaving out the last sc. Sl st
to the first sc. Fasten off. — 90 sts (10 sc, 60 dc3tog, 20 tr3tog); the two
tr3tog with ch 4 between them are the new petal tips

**R16** (sky blue): standing FPsc around the first dc3tog of any petal (the
one after the sc). \*Hdc in the next ch-2 sp, FPdc around the next dc3tog,
2 dc in the next ch-2 sp, FPdc around the next dc3tog, 3 dc in the next ch-3
sp, FPdc around the tr3tog, 7 dc in the ch-4 sp, FPdc around the next tr3tog,
3 dc in the next ch-3 sp, FPdc around the dc3tog, 2 dc in the next ch-2 sp,
FPdc around the dc3tog, hdc in the next ch-2 sp, FPsc around the last dc3tog;
pass the ch 3, sc, ch 3 between petals; FPsc around the first dc3tog of the
next petal\*; rep from \* around, leaving out the last FPsc. Sl st to the
first FPsc. Fasten off. — 270 sts (27 per petal)

**R17** (royal blue), all in the third loop: start in the first dc of any
petal, which is the 4th stitch (after FPsc, hdc, FPdc). Standing sc in its
third loop. \*Sc in the third loop of each of the next 9 sts, 2 sc in the
third loop of the next st (the middle dc of the 7), sc in the third loop of
each of the next 10 sts, ch 4, skip 6 sts (FPdc, hdc, FPsc, FPsc, hdc, FPdc),
sc in the third loop of the first dc of the next petal\*; rep from \* around,
leaving out the last sc. Sl st to the first sc. Fasten off. — 220 sc, 10 ch-4
sp. Check: after the first 10 sc you should be exactly at the middle of the
7-dc fan, with 3 dc either side.

**R18** (light grey), sc in BLO: count the 22 sc of a petal 1 to 22. Standing
sc BLO in sc 4, sc BLO in each of sc 5–11. \*Ch 2 (the petal tip), sc BLO in
each of sc 12–19, ch 2, in the ch-4 sp: dc3tog, ch 3, dc5tog, ch 3, dc3tog;
ch 2, skip sc 20–22 and sc 1–3 of the next petal, sc BLO in each of sc
4–11\*; rep from \* around, leaving out the last 8 sc. Sl st to the first sc.
Fasten off. — 190 sts (160 sc, 20 dc3tog, 10 dc5tog), 30 ch-2 sp, 20 ch-3 sp.
The three clusters are a squeeze in one ch-4 space; shuffle them along to
make room.

**R19** (aqua): the runs of 8 sc from R18 are left alone; they stay as
pointed petals in front, and this round arcs over them. Standing BPsc around
the middle dc of the 7-dc fan of R16 at any petal tip: reach behind R17 and
R18, so the BPsc is hidden behind the tip and holds it. \*Ch 7, 3 sc in the
next ch-2 sp (after the second run of 8 sc), FPsc around the dc3tog, 5 sc in
the ch-3 sp, FPsc around the dc5tog, 5 sc in the next ch-3 sp, FPsc around the
dc3tog, 3 sc in the next ch-2 sp, ch 7, BPsc around the middle R16 dc of the
next petal\*; rep from \* around, leaving out the last BPsc. Sl st to the
first BPsc. Fasten off. — 200 sts, 20 ch-7 sp

## Finishing

1. Weave in the ends: two per round, 38 in all. Easier a few rounds at a
   time than all at the end *(ours)*.
2. Block it. Mandalas need it: wet or spray it, pin it out flat in a circle
   with the petal tips and ch-7 loops pulled gently outward, and let it dry.
   This opens the lace and settles rounds that bunch
   ([how](../../knowledge/techniques/finishing.md#blocking)). The tutorial
   goes straight from weaving in to the hoop; blocking is our step.
3. Mount it. Pick a hoop about 10 cm wider than the mandala, because it
   stretches a lot: 50 cm for the 40 cm DK version. The tutorial doesn't show
   the lacing. We'd use a long length of matching yarn, going through each
   ch-7 loop and petal tip and around the hoop, tensioning evenly all round
   before tying off *(ours, untested)*. The thumbnail's hoop is wrapped in
   yarn first; optional.

## Tips from the source

- **R1: count as you go.** Stop when there are 10 posts and 10 spaces; it's
  easy to overshoot.
- **R3: find the tops of the trebles** by tugging them, and don't land in the
  ch-1 next to one.
- **Back post stitches are fiddlier than front post.** Go slowly round the
  back of the post.
- **R15 is the one to keep the chain counts beside you for.**
- **The third loop (R17) is fiddly but worth it.** It takes a while to find
  the loop on each stitch; the ridge it leaves is the point.
- **Check yourself in R17:** 10 sc should land you dead centre of the fan.
- **Choose the hoop after you've finished**, about 10 cm bigger than the
  mandala measures flat.

## Variations

- **One ball instead of 19 ends.** The tutorial's other two mandalas are a
  self-striping yarn with short colour repeats and a gradient yarn; both work
  with the standing-stitch starts kept or swapped for starting chains.
- **The designer's original** is fingering cotton on a 3.5 mm hook: a smaller,
  finer mandala. Size not given.
- **Stop early** *(ours, untested)*: after R8 for a flat flower, or after R17
  for a scalloped mat without the pointed edge. Don't stop between R10 and
  R13: the R10 petal tips are loose until R14 catches them.
