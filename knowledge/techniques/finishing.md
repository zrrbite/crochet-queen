# Finishing

## Fastening off

Cut the yarn, leaving a tail of about 15 cm (6 in). Yo and pull the tail all
the way through the loop on the hook, then tug to tighten.

## Invisible join (needle join)

Ends a round without the bump a slip stitch join leaves, so the edge looks
continuous. Use it in place of the final sl st join.

1. Work the last stitch of the round. Don't join. Cut the yarn and pull the
   tail through the loop on the hook.
2. Thread the tail onto a tapestry needle.
3. Find the first real stitch of the round (skip a starting chain if it
   doesn't count). Pass the needle under both of its top loops, front to back.
4. Bring the needle back down into the top of the last stitch, through the
   centre of its V, where the tail came out.
5. Pull gently until the new loop is the same size as the other V's along
   the edge. Don't pull tight, or it puckers.

## Weaving in ends

- Use a tapestry (blunt, large-eye) needle.
- Work on the back (wrong side), running the tail through the backs of the
  stitches so it doesn't show on the front.
- Change direction at least once. A tail that goes one way only can pull out.
- Try not to split the yarn of the stitches you pass through, though with
  thick yarn it matters less.
- On things that will be washed often, like coasters and dishcloths, a couple
  of small knots on the back before trimming help the end survive the
  machine. A dot of fabric glue is another option.

## Blocking

Fresh crochet is often a little misshapen: a circle that domes, a square
that's slightly off. Blocking sets it into shape.

**Spray blocking**, gentle and fine for most yarns:

1. Lay the piece flat on a towel, right side up, and pat it into shape.
2. Mist it lightly with water until damp, not soaked.
3. Lay another towel or cloth on top and weigh it down with something flat
   (a few books). You only need enough weight to hold it flat.
4. Leave it until completely dry, usually overnight.

**Don't iron crochet**, acrylic in particular: direct heat flattens the
stitches for good and can melt the fibre.
