# Increases and decreases

How a piece grows and shrinks. Flat circles, hats and amigurumi are all
shaped by where these go; see [flat circles](../techniques/flat-circles.md)
for spreading them evenly.

## Increase (inc)

Two stitches in the same stitch. In a single-crochet pattern, `inc` means
2 sc in one stitch; in a double-crochet pattern, 2 dc. The pattern's main
stitch is the one to double.

## Single crochet decrease (sc2tog, dec)

Two stitches worked together into one.

1. Insert hook into the next stitch, yo, pull up a loop (2 loops on hook).
2. Insert hook into the following stitch, yo, pull up a loop (3 loops).
3. Yo, pull through all 3 loops.

## Invisible decrease (invdec)

The usual decrease in amigurumi. It leaves almost no gap or bump, so the
stuffing doesn't peek through.

1. Insert hook under the **front loop only** of the next stitch, then
   straight on under the front loop of the stitch after it (3 loops on hook).
2. Yo, pull through the 2 front loops (2 loops left).
3. Yo, pull through both.

Near the end of a stuffed piece, when the opening is small and front loops
are hard to reach, it's fine to switch to an ordinary sc2tog.

## Double crochet decrease (dc2tog)

1. Yo, insert into the next stitch, yo, pull up a loop, yo, pull through 2
   (2 loops on hook).
2. Yo, insert into the following stitch, yo, pull up a loop, yo, pull
   through 2 (3 loops).
3. Yo, pull through all 3.
